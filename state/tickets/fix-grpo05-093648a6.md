# Fix grpo05: OOM (ran out of memory)

## Issue
The grpo05 job (40-step 0.5B CPU GRPO, 33 GB job cap) dies by OOM: the 10-11 run
finished step 5 (runs/grpo05_exercism/metrics.jsonl, ckpt at step 5) and never wrote
step 6; the 10-12 re-dispatch (job/grpo05 merge 12:40) failed the same way. This
blocks the CPU GRPO result the report depends on. The grpomem fix (micro_batch=8,
cap 24->33) is in the job branch, so this OOM is post-fix.

## Question
Why does the job's 33 GB cgroup overflow when the grpomem probe peaked at 25.1 GiB?
Hypothesis: the probe measured parent-process ru_maxrss only (25.1 GiB, /usr/bin/time
-v), but the cap counts the whole cgroup — the 8 concurrent pytest grader subprocesses
(train.py:271 grade_all, max_workers=8) and the job's 12 OMP threads, so cgroup anon
= parent + graders > 33 GB. Refuting observation: a capped-style run (parent + all
children anon sampled) from the step-5 checkpoint completing steps 6-10 with peak
< 33 GB.

  rollout 24-29 GB (no_grad prefill [8,14,T,T]+[8,T,V] fp32, T~1200-1600);
  training-backward spikes 50-54 GB (mb=8, T~1584: 24 layers of [8,14,T,T]

  73.5 GB is COW-inflated (summing children's Anonymous double-counts fork
  pages) — PEAKMEM is the reliable number. The 25.1 GiB probe was a 2-step
  short-sequence run: unrepresentative, backward scales with B*T^2. The job
  died on its first wide step (its step 6); my 8-thread run peaked on step 1
  (tiny weight diff -> wide completions earlier).
